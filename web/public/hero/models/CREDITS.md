# Models

Every object on the landing page is built from geometry in
`web/src/landing/three/objects.tsx`: the carrier, its ice packs, the vaccine
carton, the sensor puck, the location tag and the vial.

Free models were evaluated first (Poly Pizza's CC0 "Cooler Box" by
MilkAndBanana and "Cardboard Boxes" by Quaternius, plus low-poly vials and
bottles). They are low-poly game assets with uncontrolled interiors, and a
clean filleted geometry with real materials reads closer to a product render,
so none are shipped.
